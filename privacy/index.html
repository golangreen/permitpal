Permit Pal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Permit Pal is made by Green Day Creations ("we", "us"). Contact: support@punchlee.com

Who it is for

Permit Pal is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password, which our sign-in provider stores in protected form.
  • Profile: your first name, whether you are a teen or a parent, your state, your birth year (used only to check you are 13 or older), and your family code.
  • Drive log: start and end time, minutes driven, night minutes, miles, the supervisor's name, optional notes, and whether a parent approved it.
  • Purchases: whether you have Permit Pal Pro. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • Quiz progress is kept on your phone only.

Location

Permit Pal uses your location only while a drive is running, to measure miles and to work out sunset for night hours. We do not save your route and no coordinates leave your phone. Only the mileage total and the night minutes result are saved.

How we use it

Only to run the app: your account, your drive log, parent approval and Pro access. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or sites, we do not use analytics, and we never sell or rent your information.

Who handles it for us

Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. RevenueCat manages purchase status together with Apple and Google. They process data only on our behalf.

Who can see your drives

Members of your family group. Parents can see their teen's name, state and drives. Nobody outside your family group can.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete your account. In the app, open Me and tap Delete my account to erase your profile, drives and quiz progress. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules limit each account to its own family's records.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing support@punchlee.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

Permit Pal is not affiliated with any DMV or government agency.
