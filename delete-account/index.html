Delete your Permit Pal account

Permit Pal is made by Green Day Creations.

In the app

1. Open Permit Pal and sign in.
2. Tap the Me tab.
3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete.

Your account and data are erased right away.

Without the app

Email support@punchlee.com from the email address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in email, first name, birth year, state, family link, every drive you logged, and your quiz and lesson progress.

What is kept

Nothing in Permit Pal. Copies in our database backups expire on their own within 30 days. If you bought Pro, Apple or Google keeps its own purchase record, and you cancel a subscription in your Apple or Google account settings, not in Permit Pal.
